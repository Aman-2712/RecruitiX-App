# Hirecue: Future Roadmap & Growth Strategy

## Part 1: Feature Updates Tiered by Subscription Plan
To drive revenue, we need to create a "fear of missing out" (FOMO) for the higher-tier plans. Here is a roadmap of powerful features we can build and restrict to specific plans.

### 🌟 STARTER PLAN (The Basics)
*Target: Small startups hiring for 1-2 roles.*
* **Basic AI Resume Parsing:** Extracts skills and education.
* **Standard Match Scoring:** Gives a percentage fit (0-100%).
* **Email Notifications:** Basic email alerts when a resume is uploaded.
* **Limit:** 1 User, 50 Resumes/month.

### 🚀 GROWTH PLAN (The Sweet Spot - Most Popular)
*Target: Medium businesses and HR agencies.*
* **AI Cover Letter Analysis:** The AI also reads and scores cover letters.
* **Custom Screening Questions:** Recruiters can add custom questions (e.g., "Must have React experience?"), and the AI acts as a filter.
* **Automated Rejection Emails:** Connect an email account to automatically send polite "Unfortunately we went with another candidate" emails to low-scoring resumes.
* **Export to CSV:** Download the ranked candidate list to Excel.
* **Limit:** 5 Users, 500 Resumes/month.

### 🏢 ENTERPRISE PLAN (The Ultimate Powerhouse)
*Target: Large corporations with high volume.*
* **Interview Question Generator:** For the top 5 candidates, the AI generates personalized interview questions based on the weaknesses it found in their specific resume.
* **Bias-Free Mode:** Hides the candidate's name, gender, and age to promote diversity and fair hiring.
* **Team Collaboration:** Ability for multiple recruiters to leave comments and vote on a resume.
* **API Access:** Connect Hirecue directly to their existing HR software (like Workday or BambooHR).
* **Limit:** Unlimited Users, Custom Resume Volume.

## Part 2: B2B Marketing Strategy (How to get clients)

Since Hirecue is a B2B (Business-to-Business) tool, you aren't selling to normal consumers. You are selling to **Recruiters, HR Managers, and Startup Founders**. Here is the strategy to land them.

### 1. The "Cold Email" Strategy (Direct Outreach)
This is the fastest way to get your first 10 clients.
* **Who to target:** Startup Founders and HR Managers on LinkedIn who recently posted "We are hiring!"
* **What to say:** Keep it incredibly short. 
  > *"Hi [Name], saw you are hiring for a Software Engineer. You're probably getting flooded with resumes. I built an AI tool that instantly ranks the top 5 candidates from a pile of PDFs. Can I give you a 1-month free trial to use on this job post?"*
* **The Goal:** Give away free trials to your first 10 users to get testimonials.

### 2. LinkedIn Content Creation (The Best Social Media for B2B)
Do not use Instagram or TikTok. Your buyers (HR managers) hang out on LinkedIn.
* **Content Type 1 (The Problem):** Talk about how broken hiring is. *"Recruiters spend 15 hours a week reading bad resumes. Here is how AI is fixing it."*
* **Content Type 2 (The Product Demo):** Post 30-second screen recordings of you dragging 50 resumes into Hirecue and getting the results in 10 seconds. Show, don't just tell!
* **Content Type 3 (The Case Study):** Once you have a client, post their results. *"Company X reduced their hiring time by 80% using our AI screener."*

### 3. Product Hunt Launch
Product Hunt is a website where early adopters look for new software. 
* Prepare a beautiful launch video and screenshots.
* Launch at 12:01 AM PST on a Tuesday or Wednesday.
* Offer a special "Product Hunt Discount" (e.g., 50% off the Growth Plan for life).

### 4. Create "Free Tools" for SEO
Build a small, free tool on your website to drive Google search traffic.
* Example: A free "Resume ATS Checker" for applicants. Applicants upload their resume to see if it passes your AI. This drives massive traffic to your domain, and the businesses hiring them will notice your brand.
